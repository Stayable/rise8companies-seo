02 · Subordinate Debt

Mezzanine
Financing.

Subordinate debt that fills the gap between senior financing and equity — structured co-terminus with the senior loan.

Mezzanine sits between the senior mortgage and equity in the capital structure. Secured by a pledge of ownership interests rather than a direct lien on the property, it gives owners access to leverage that conventional financing alone cannot provide. Interest only or fixed principal paydowns — structured to the deal.

Typical Use Cases
Supplementing agency senior financing at closing
Existing mortgage loan augmentation
Reducing equity requirement on stabilized assets
Recapitalization of existing capital structures

03 · Junior Capital

Preferred
Equity.

Junior capital that sits in the ownership structure rather than the debt stack — maximum leverage with the lightest DSCR threshold.

Preferred equity sits inside the borrower's organizational structure rather than as a lien on the real estate. It offers the highest combined LTV of any RISE8 product — up to 90% — with a minimum DSCR as low as 1.05x through the preferred payments. Used in conjunction with new or existing agency mortgage financing.

Typical Use Cases
Maximizing leverage on stabilized multifamily
New multifamily construction with agency senior debt
Supplementing existing financing at the ownership level
Structured alongside agency debt requiring co-termination

Our Approach

RISE8 Real Estate Finance is the structured financing arm of RISE8 Companies — a vertically integrated real estate operating company that owns and manages multifamily properties across six Florida markets.

We don't underwrite from a spreadsheet. We underwrite from an operator's perspective — we know what it costs to turn a unit, what a deferred maintenance schedule looks like at exit, and what a true lease-up curve feels like from the inside. That context changes how we structure deals.

Certainty of execution is the product. We know the asset class, we move quickly, and we don't retrade.
