The standard RISE8 section opener: a left-column kicker and a large serif headline over a 1px ink top rule. Wrap one phrase of the headline in `<em>` for the teal italic accent.

```jsx
<SectionHead kicker="02 · Our platform">
  Four operating companies under <em>one parent.</em>
</SectionHead>
```

Use `onDark` on navy sections. Keep to one `<em>` per headline.
